# Resident Question Bank

These questions are for residents to describe the specialty as it actually behaves in the local environment.

Use them to capture reality, not preference.

## General context

- What specialty are you in?
- Which hospital do you train in?
- Which wilaya are you training in?
- What year of residency are you in? (R1-R5)
- Optional: what is your gender?

## Work schedule

- About how many hours do you work per week?
- About how many consultations do you do per day?
- About how many patients do you see per day?
- About how many surgeries do you do per week, if your specialty is surgical?
- How many night shifts do you do per month?
- How many weekend work days do you do per month?
- How often are you on call?
- On an average guard, how many hours of sleep do you get?

## Clinical reality

- How often do emergencies occur in your specialty?
  - Never
  - Rarely
  - Weekly
  - Daily
  - Constantly
- During emergencies, how stressful is the work?
- What percentage of your work is:
  - Surgery
  - Procedures
  - Consultations
  - Paperwork
- How much long-term follow-up does your specialty require?
- How much patient communication is required?
- How much family communication is required?

## Knowledge and skill demands

- How important is anatomy in your specialty?
- How important is physiology in your specialty?
- How important is pharmacology in your specialty?
- How important is imaging in your specialty?
- How important is manual dexterity in your specialty?
- How important is decision-making in your specialty?
- How important is pattern recognition in your specialty?
- How important is communication in your specialty?
- How important is leadership in your specialty?

## Personality fit

- How suitable is this specialty for someone who is introverted?
- How suitable is this specialty for someone who is extroverted?
- How suitable is this specialty for someone who is calm?
- How suitable is this specialty for someone who is competitive?
- How suitable is this specialty for someone who is detail-oriented?
- How suitable is this specialty for someone who is creative?
- How suitable is this specialty for someone who is analytical?
- How suitable is this specialty for someone who is patient?
- How suitable is this specialty for someone who is decisive?
- How suitable is this specialty for someone who is comfortable under pressure?

## Algeria-specific reality

- How strong is the demand for this specialty in Algeria?
- How competitive is this specialty at the residency exam?
- How available is the equipment needed for this specialty?
- How good is the training quality in Algeria?
- How strong is the future demand for this specialty?
- How realistic are rural opportunities in this specialty?
- How realistic are urban opportunities in this specialty?
- How often would someone need to go abroad for fellowship or advanced training?
- How easy is it to open a private practice in this specialty?
- How heavy is the administrative burden in this specialty?

## Residency environment

- How difficult is residency in this specialty?
- How many hours does residency usually demand?
- How good is the teaching quality?
- How many operating opportunities do residents get?
- How much autonomy do residents have?
